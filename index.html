Delper · Transaction Flow

From ad click to vendor payout

An 18-step sequence in which the buyer's USD 300 is held in escrow, inspected by a third-party quality controller, and only then released to the seller.

Phase 1: Catalogue ingestion and discovery (free funnel)

Sellers join at no cost. The platform indexes their catalogue and bids for buyer attention.

  1. 1Free catalogue ingestionThe seller connects their storefront (Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, ONDC, or Proxtera). The middleware syncs the full inventory to the platform at no cost.
  2. 2Passive traffic monitoringUnsponsored products are monitored in "Ghost Mode," tracking real-time search trends and indexing metadata for search engines without running active ads.
  3. 3Programmatic RTB matchingThe ad-tech engine identifies a high-intent shopper on a partner network and runs a real-time bidding sequence to show the seller's item.
  4. 4The conversion hookThe buyer clicks the ad and starts a purchase of the USD 300 product, showing immediate intent to buy.

Phase 2: Gateway interception and paywall

The platform holds the order before it reaches the seller's warehouse dashboard.

  1. 5Checkout interceptionMiddleware intercepts the native payment routing and pauses checkout before the order reaches the seller's dashboard.
  2. 6Subscription paywall activationThe seller receives an automated alert: a buyer is ready to lock USD 300 into escrow, and the seller must pay the USD 100 annual subscription to claim it.
  3. 7Access clearanceThe seller pays the USD 100 annual fee, which upgrades the account to Premium Active and unlocks the escrow routing pipeline.

Phase 3: Escrow isolation and upfront quality controls

The buyer's funds are locked before anything ships.

  1. 8Escrow fund lockupThe buyer's USD 300 is authorized and routed into a locked Stripe Connect custodial escrow vault. The buyer pays no processing or trust fees.
  2. 9Upfront 3PQC prepaymentThe seller prepays third-party quality control (3PQC) and logistics fees at cost, about USD 15, through the dashboard.
  3. 10Leg 1: inbound logisticsThe system generates a shipping label. The seller ships the item to the nearest SGS or QIMA inspection hub, not to the buyer's address.

Phase 4: The physical trust gate

Inspectors decide whether the item moves on to the buyer.

  1. 113PQC inspection gateSGS technicians unbox the product and run a five-point checklist covering authenticity, cosmetic flaws, and structural functionality.
    If the product failsThe order is cancelled. The buyer receives an immediate USD 300 refund from escrow. The item is returned to the seller using the prepaid logistics funds, so the platform loses USD 0.
    If the product passesThe item is certified "Flawless," sealed in an official box with a tamper-evident digital seal, and a cryptographic "Passed" token is sent to the platform through the SGS API.

Phase 5: Doorstep delivery and buyer sign-off

Only items with a "Passed" token leave the hub.

  1. 12Leg 2: outbound logisticsOn detecting the "Passed" token, the middleware releases the item from the hub and ships it to the buyer's doorstep, pulling live carrier tracking.
  2. 13Buyer Satisfaction Certificate (BSC)The buyer unboxes the item and submits a digitally signed BSC through the mobile or web widget.
  3. 1472-hour "deemed satisfaction" overrideIf the buyer does not sign or dispute, a background job checks carrier data. After 72 hours from delivery with no dispute, the system issues a system-signed satisfaction token so the seller's cash flow is not blocked.

Phase 6: Day 16 financial clearing

Payout follows a fixed 16-day timetable after the satisfaction token.

  1. 15T-minus 16 day countdownThe satisfaction token from step 13 or 14 starts a 16-day payout sequence in the Stripe Connect automation engine.
  2. 16Platform revenue sweep (22.2% + 2%)At 12:00:01 AM on day 16, the escrow engine transfers a lead generation fee (USD 66.67, used to pay for RTB ad inventory) and a 2% sales commission (USD 6) to the platform's operating account, USD 72.67 in total.
  3. 17Vendor disbursalThe remaining USD 227.33 is paid to the seller's linked bank account or digital wallet.

Fee basis. Seller-paid fees on a USD 300 order total USD 72.67 (24.2%): a USD 66.67 lead-generation fee (22.2%) and a USD 6 sales commission (2%). This matches the Delper overview.

The Phase 5 timing (72-hour override and day-16 payout) and the "USD 0 platform loss" on a failed inspection assume the return logistics are fully covered by the seller's prepayment. Check that against actual 3PQC and shipping costs.
